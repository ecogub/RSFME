2026-07-27 | M3a: Unified Figure Style Established
- Created source/plot_theme.R with theme_rsfme(), method/solute/season palettes,
  error band scales, and ggsave_hess() helper.
- Based on HESS submission requirements: 300 DPI, sans-serif (Helvetica), single-col
  8cm / double-col 17.4cm, colorblind-safe (Paul Tol qualitative scheme).
- Method palette: blue=#4477AA (LI), red=#EE6677 (Beale), green=#228833 (Rating),
  yellow=#CCBB44 (Composite), black=#000000 (Truth).
- Error bands: green=#228833 (±5%), yellow=#CCBB44 (±20%).
- Panel labels should use lowercase (a), (b) per HESS style.
